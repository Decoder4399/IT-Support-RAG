# Email Troubleshooting Guide

## Overview
This guide covers common email issues and their solutions for Outlook, Webmail, and mobile email clients.

## Common Symptoms
- Cannot send or receive emails
- Emails stuck in outbox
- Outlook keeps asking for password
- Calendar not syncing
- Missing emails or folders
- Email very slow to load

## Outlook Desktop Client Issues

### Step 1: Check Connection Status
- Look at the bottom right of Outlook window
- If it says "Disconnected" or "Trying to connect..."
- Click on the connection status and select "Reconnect"

### Step 2: Restart Outlook
1. Save any open drafts
2. Close Outlook completely (check Task Manager to ensure it's closed)
3. Wait 10 seconds
4. Reopen Outlook

### Step 3: Check Mailbox Size
1. Click File > Mailbox Settings
2. Check "Mailbox Size"
3. If over 45 GB, archive old emails:
   - File > Tools > Clean Up Old Items
   - Select "Archive this folder and all subfolders"
   - Choose a date range (archive anything older than 1 year)

### Step 4: Repair Outlook Profile
1. Close Outlook
2. Open Control Panel > Mail
3. Click "Show Profiles"
4. Select your profile and click "Properties"
5. Click "Email Accounts"
6. Select your email account and click "Repair"
7. Follow the repair wizard
8. Restart Outlook

## Webmail Issues (Office 365)

### Cannot Access Webmail
1. Go to https://outlook.office.com
2. Ensure you're using your company email address
3. Check if your password is correct
4. Clear browser cache and cookies
5. Try a different browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)

### Emails Not Showing in Webmail
1. Click the refresh button in the top right
2. Check the "Other" or "Focused" inbox tabs
3. Search for the missing email by sender or subject
4. Check the "Deleted Items" and "Junk Email" folders

## Mobile Email Issues

### iOS (iPhone/iPad)
1. Go to Settings > Mail > Accounts
2. Select your work account
3. Toggle "Mail" off and on
4. If still not working, delete the account and re-add it

### Android
1. Open the Email app
2. Go to Settings > Accounts
3. Select your work account
4. Tap "Sync Now"
5. If still not working, remove the account and re-add it

## Calendar and Sync Issues

### Calendar Not Syncing
1. In Outlook, go to File > Options > Calendar
2. Ensure "Auto-Archive" is not hiding appointments
3. Check the calendar is selected (checkbox next to it)
4. Try forcing a sync: Send/Receive > Update Folders

### Contacts Not Syncing to Phone
1. Ensure you're syncing the correct folder
2. In mobile settings, check sync frequency (set to 15 minutes or manual)
3. Try removing and re-adding the account on your phone

## Email Security

### Suspicious Emails
1. Do NOT click any links or open attachments
2. Forward the email as an attachment to security@company.com
3. Delete the email from your inbox
4. Report it using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook

### Email Account Compromised
1. Change your password immediately
2. Check for rules: Outlook > Manage Rules & Alerts
3. Remove any suspicious forwarding rules
4. Check Sent Items for emails you didn't send
5. Report to IT Security immediately

## When to Escalate
- Email account is completely inaccessible
- Suspected security breach or compromise
- Need shared mailbox access
- Email forwarding rules you didn't create
- Calendar events disappearing
- Legal hold or compliance issues
